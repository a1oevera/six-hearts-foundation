How donating works

Four steps between your spare room and someone who needs help.

Step one

Snap photos

Email us pictures of the gently used items you're parting with, plus a quick description.

Step two

We post & sell

We list your items and handle the selling — you don't have to deal with any of it.

Step three

You choose the share

When an item sells, you decide what portion of the sale goes to the cause we're supporting.

Step four

Easy pickup

Buyers pick the item up directly from you, or from us — whichever is easier.

How Sixhearts started

Sixhearts began small: selling a few friends' unused things and giving the money to charity. Homes got lighter, and the money did real good — so we're scaling up. Now we help families across our community declutter, and the proceeds go to a rotating cause.

Sixhearts Foundation is an officially registered non-profit, and we're working toward CRA charitable registration.
